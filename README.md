# AEGIS
## Your Personal AI Assistant — Private, Free, Open-Source

Built for Laurence Coburn · Mobile-first · iPhone 16 / iPad Air optimized

---

### WHAT IS AEGIS
AEGIS is your all-in-one AI workspace — private, self-hosted, and fully yours. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no third-party controlling your data. It grows with you — simple to start, powerful to expand.

### FEATURES
- 📱 **Mobile-first** — designed perfectly for iPhone & iPad
- 🔒 **100% private** — your data stays on your setup
- 🆓 **Forever free** — open-source, no paid tiers required
- 🤖 **Cloudflare AI powered** — Llama 3.3 built-in, works instantly
- 🎤 **Voice-ready** — add speech-to-text anytime
- 📁 **File storage ready** — connect documents & projects
- 🧠 **Memory & learning** — grows smarter the more you use it
- 💻 **No zips, no drag-drop** — edit directly on GitHub, copy-paste deploy
